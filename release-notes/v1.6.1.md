### 💻 Runs on Intel Macs again
- One universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 15 Sequoia or later, in place of the per-chip downloads. Releases after 1.2.3 had no Intel build, and needed macOS 26.
- `claude-persona update` finds the new download on either kind of Mac, from any installed version, 1.2.3 included.

### 🔏 Checked before it ships
- A release refuses a binary missing either architecture, built for a newer macOS, or linking a library outside the system, before it tags anything.
